## Reproducibility

The figures accompany the analysis in [`predictive_maintenance_clustering.ipynb`](../notebooks/predictive_maintenance_clustering.ipynb). Numerical findings should be interpreted alongside the notebook and the published research report.

The figures summarize an exploratory analysis of the synthetic AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset. They do not establish causal relationships or demonstrate a validated industrial predictive-maintenance system.
